# Maintainers

NSpark is stewarded by **OrkLabs S.A.S.**

## Active maintainers

| GitHub | Focus area |
|---|---|
| [@realbub](https://github.com/realbub) | Lead maintainer / releases |

## Responsibilities

- Triage incoming issues, discussions, and pull requests.
- Review and merge contributions in line with [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- Tag and publish releases. See `docs/release-process.md` *(forthcoming)*.
- Respond to security disclosures within the SLA defined in
  [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

## How to escalate

- Maintainer unreachable for ≥ 7 days on a critical issue: email
  **gm@orklabs.com**.
- Conduct concerns: email **gm@orklabs.com** with `[NSpark Conduct]` in
  the subject (see [`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)).
- Security bugs: see [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).
